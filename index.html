Architectural Mechanics

  • 3-Stream Conv3D: Alternating 3x3x3 and 5x5x5 kernels with residual skip connections.
  • 3D CBAM: Dynamic channel attention and 3D spatial attention map extraction.
  • Adaptive Focal Loss: Binary focal loss (γ = 2.0, α = 0.25) preventing class collapse.
